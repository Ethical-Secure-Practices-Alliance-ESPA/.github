Welcome to the official GitHub organization for the **Ethical & Secure Practices Alliance (ESPA)** – a Swiss nonprofit dedicated to promoting **ethical, secure, and responsible practices in digital technologies**.

---

## 🌐 About ESPA

ESPA unites professionals, researchers, and organizations to advance **cybersecurity, ethical standards, and responsible technology use**.  

**Our Mission:**
- Advocate for secure and ethical digital systems.
- Provide resources, guidelines, and open-source tools.
- Foster collaboration and knowledge sharing in technology and security.
- Support education, research, and community-led initiatives.
